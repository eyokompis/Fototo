Help

Open a photo

Click Open photo or drag an image file onto the window. Recently opened photos appear as thumbnails on the start screen.

Adjust

The four sliders update the preview instantly:

  • Exposure — brightens or darkens the whole photo
  • Contrast — separates lights and darks
  • Saturation — color intensity (0 is black & white)
  • Temperature — warmer (orange) or cooler (blue) tones

Edits are saved automatically per photo and restored when you reopen it. Reset returns the sliders to neutral. Your original file is never modified.

Upscale 4× (AI)

Increases the resolution 4× with Real-ESRGAN. The AI model (~5 MB) downloads once on first use; an internet connection is only needed for that download.

Enhance light (AI)

Brightens dark or underexposed photos with Zero-DCE. This model is included in the app.

Rotate & crop

⟲ / ⟳ rotate the photo by 90°. Crop lets you drag a rectangle on the photo — click Apply crop to keep only that part, or Cancel to discard. The original file is never modified; use Save to export the result.

Gallery

Click Gallery to browse every photo in your library. Add folder imports all images from a folder at once (thumbnails appear once a photo has been opened).

Batch processing

In the Gallery, click Select and choose the photos you want, then pick Upscale 4× or Enhance light. Choose an output folder and each processed copy is saved there as name-fototo.png — originals are never modified.

Save

Exports the edited photo as a new PNG file (name-fototo.png); the original stays untouched.

Privacy

Everything runs locally on your machine. Photos are never uploaded anywhere.
